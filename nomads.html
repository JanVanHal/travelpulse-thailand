Digital nomads

One to six months, or longer on the Destination Thailand Visa. Remote work, a Thai stretch, and the paperwork that sits behind it.

Last checked: 2026-10-21

Who this is for

You work remotely, freelance, or you are here for Thai soft power (Muay Thai, cooking, medical). You need more than a 30-day stamp. You still travel light: a laptop, insurance that lasts, and a plan for money without a Thai bank account.

Visa route: Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

The DTV is a 5-year multiple-entry visa. You may stay up to 180 days per entry, then extend once by 180 days at immigration. After that you leave and re-enter.

Purposes listed by Thai e-Visa: workcation (remote workers and freelancers), Thai soft power (Muay Thai, cooking, medical), and spouse or children of holders.

  • Bank balance of at least 500,000 THB. Many embassies ask for the last 3 months of statements.
  • Proof of employment or a portfolio.
  • A criminal record certificate, now asked by several embassies.
  • Apply in your country of nationality or residence.
  • Fee: 10,000 THB, or the local equivalent (for example 350 EUR in Helsinki, 400 USD in Washington).

Rules vary by embassy. Check your embassy before you book a flight to apply. Start at thaievisa.go.th DTV page.

Money

Kasikorn Bank states that accounts are not opened for DTV holders (nor for tourist visa, Visa on Arrival, or visa-exempt stays). You need a Non-Immigrant visa of another type. Ask the branch, because practice can vary, and you can pre-check documents via LINE @doccheck on the KBank page.

Plan on Wise, cash, and cards. Whether Wise or other foreign apps can pay Thai PromptPay QR codes still needs a check.

Banking and money

Insurance

A nomad subscription (monthly, pauseable) fits a 180-day stay better than a two-week travel policy. Whether insurance is required for the DTV depends on the visa type and the embassy.

Nomad and travel insurance

Work setup

  • eSIM or a local tourist SIM for data. A Thai number helps with some apps and OTPs.
  • Cafe and hotel wifi: treat it as public. VPNPulse covers the VPN part.
  • Northern Thailand has a smoky season around February to April. Check AQI if you are settling in Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai for those months.
  • 90-day address reporting applies to long-stay foreigners. Online and in-person options, and TM.30 landlord notification, need a check on immigration.go.th.
