Travel & digital-nomad insurance

Private hospitals in Thailand are good and they bill. Motorbikes are the clause that catches people. Pick a type, then read the wording.

Last checked: 2026-10-21

Why it matters here

A private-hospital stay is the scenario this page is for. Motorbike accidents are common, and they are often excluded unless the policy says they are covered and you hold a valid licence. Confirm that in the wording you buy. This page does not quote premiums or coverage limits.

⚠ To verify — check the official source before relying on this.

Three types

  • Short-trip travel insurance — a few weeks, one country or a region, then it ends.
  • Nomad subscription — monthly, built for people who stay or hop. SafetyWing and Genki sit in this group.
  • Expat health — annual international health cover if you are settling in.

DTV and retirement O-A: whether insurance is required depends on the visa type. Check the form you are filing.
