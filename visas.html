How long are you staying?

Pick a length. The card that matches is the route to read first. This is not legal advice, and embassy rules differ.

Last checked: 2026-10-21

Stay length

Visa exemption (from 15 September 2026)

The 60-day scheme was revoked. Nationals of 60 countries (including the UK, US, EU states, Australia, Canada and New Zealand) get 30 days for tourism.

  • Seychelles and Mauritius: 15 days.
  • Argentina, Brazil, Chile, South Korea and Peru: 90 days under bilateral agreements.
  • Visa on Arrival now applies only to Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia: 15 days, 2,000 THB.
  • Land and sea visa-exempt entries are limited to 2 per calendar year, except Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore.

You still submit a TDAC before each entry. Officers may ask for an onward ticket, accommodation, and 20,000 THB per person / 40,000 THB per family.

Tourist track

1–6 months: Destination Thailand Visa

The DTV is the usual path for a workcation of months rather than weeks. 5-year multiple entry, up to 180 days per entry, extendable once by 180 days at immigration, then leave and re-enter.

Bank balance of at least 500,000 THB (many embassies want 3 months of statements), employment or portfolio proof, and often a criminal record certificate. Apply in your country of nationality or residence. Fee 10,000 THB, or the local equivalent (350 EUR Helsinki, 400 USD Washington).

Check your embassy. thaievisa.go.th DTV.

Kasikorn Bank does not open accounts for DTV holders. Nomad track · Money

6+ months

A single DTV entry plus its 180-day extension covers up to a year, then you leave and re-enter on the same 5-year visa. Past that, people look at retirement O / O-A (50+), LTR, Thailand Privilege, marriage Non-O, or an education visa. Each of those has its own tests. Confirm on thaievisa.go.th and with the embassy that will issue it.

Retiring: Non-Immigrant O / O-A, 50+

Financial thresholds are commonly cited as an 800,000 THB deposit or 65,000 THB per month income. O-A insurance requirements are separate. Confirm both on thaievisa.go.th and immigration.go.th. Do not treat the figures on this page as the current legal test.

Extensions

You can apply to extend at immigration before your permitted stay ends, at the officer's discretion (Embassy Doha). Length and fee for a visa-exempt extension after the 2026 change need a check on immigration.go.th.

FAQ

What changed on 15 September 2026?

The 60-day exemption ended. Most of the 60 listed countries now get 30 days for tourism. VOA is only Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia.

What is the DTV?

5-year multiple entry, 180 days per stay, one 180-day extension, then leave and re-enter. 500,000 THB balance is the usual financial bar. Check your embassy.

Can I extend 30 days?

You may apply before the stay ends. Length and fee after 2026 need a current official check.
