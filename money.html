Banking & money

Who can open a Thai account, what K PLUS is for, PromptPay, and how to take cash out without extra conversion.

Last checked: 2026-10-21

Can a foreigner open a Thai account?

Kasikorn Bank's official page: foreigners need a passport plus a Non-Immigrant visa (any type) plus documents matching their purpose (work permit, school certificate, and so on). Accounts are not opened for tourist visa, Visa on Arrival, visa-exempt or DTV holders.

Source: KBank savings page. Branch practice may vary, so ask the branch. You can pre-check documents via LINE @doccheck (same page).

K PLUS

Free. You register after the account is opened. It supports passport-based customers. Use it for QR payments, transfers and bills. FAQ: K PLUS help.

PromptPay

Thailand's national QR and phone-number payment system. It is how street food, markets and a lot of rent get paid once you have a Thai account linked to it. Bank of Thailand publishes the scheme details; confirm the current consumer page on bot.or.th.

⚠ To verify — check the official source before relying on this.

Without a Thai account

Wise (multi-currency card), cash, and regular cards. Whether Wise or other foreign apps can pay a Thai PromptPay QR still needs a check. Some Thai banks have advertised tourist QR products; confirm on the bank's own page if you need that.

Other banks

Bangkok Bank, SCB and Krungsri each have their own foreigner rules, and those rules differ by branch. Ask the branch that will actually open the account.

⚠ To verify — check the official source before relying on this.

ATMs, DCC, carrying cash

  • Thai ATMs typically charge foreign cards a flat fee per withdrawal. The amount differs by bank.
  • Always decline dynamic currency conversion. Take the charge in THB.
  • Immigration may ask visa-exempt visitors to show 20,000 THB per person or 40,000 THB per family. That is a cash-on-hand figure, not a daily spending guide.
  • Change a small amount at the airport if you need taxi cash. Use a city exchanger for the rest.
