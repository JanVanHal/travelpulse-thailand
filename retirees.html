Retirees & expats

Long-stay in Thailand on a Non-Immigrant O or O-A, age 50 and over. Banking, reporting, and health cover sit next to the visa.

Last checked: 2026-10-21

Who this is for

You are settling in, not passing through. You need a visa that can be extended, a way to hold Thai baht, and a plan for hospitals. This page does not cover marriage, education, or Privilege visas beyond a one-line pointer.

Visa route: Non-Immigrant O / O-A (50+)

Retirement stays commonly use Non-Immigrant O (issued outside or converted in Thailand) or O-A (long-stay, applied from abroad). Financial thresholds are commonly cited as an 800,000 THB deposit or 65,000 THB per month income. O-A has insurance requirements. Confirm both on thaievisa.go.th and immigration.go.th before you apply. Embassy practice differs.

Stay-length picker

90-day reporting and TM.30

Long-stay foreigners must report their address to immigration every 90 days. The TDAC terms page mentions this. Online and in-person options, and TM.30 (the landlord's address notification), need a current check on immigration.go.th.

⚠ To verify — check the official source before relying on this.

Money

Kasikorn Bank opens accounts for foreigners who hold a Non-Immigrant visa of any type, plus a passport and documents that match the purpose. That is the door DTV and tourist stamps do not open. Pre-check papers via LINE @doccheck. Branch practice may still vary, so ask the branch.

Once the account exists, K PLUS is free to register and supports passport-based customers. It is the usual way to pay by QR, transfer, and pay bills. PromptPay (phone-number or national-ID QR) is how markets, street food, and a lot of rent get paid.

Insurance and healthcare

O-A insurance requirements need a check on the current official pages. Private hospitals are where most expats go for anything serious; public hospitals are cheaper and busier. Pharmacies are widespread for simple meds. Keep a policy that states motorbike, pre-existing conditions, deductible, and evacuation in plain language.
